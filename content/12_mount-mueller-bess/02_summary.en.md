# 08-18 Meeting: Mount Mueller Battery Storage Facility Project

The Mount Mueller project proposes a 400 MW, four-hour battery storage facility at 525 Reed Road near Gladstone. The pre-lodgement meeting focused on approvals, ecological constraints and the treatment of its transmission connection.

- **Approval pathway:** The transmission line is unlikely to qualify as ancillary to the BESS because it crosses marine and vegetated areas. A separate application is the working approach; a ministerial determination could potentially consolidate components.
- **Siting and ecology:** The battery footprint uses previously disturbed land to avoid threatened ecological communities. Final transmission routing, landowner consents, marine plant impacts and any EPBC referral remain key inputs.
- **Submission package:** The source calls for RPD completion before MCU submission, detailed spatial data, technical assessments and a lifecycle decommissioning security framework. Concurrent lodgement of known operational works is preferred.
- **Technical requirements discussed:** Preliminary fire safety and desktop risk studies are acceptable at lodgement. The meeting records a 0.2% AEP flood design expectation with climate change, plus geotechnical, acid sulfate soil and MUSIC water-quality assessments.
- **Next steps:** Confirm the line route, document consultation with QFES/QFD, Powerlink and transport stakeholders, await SARA’s written coastal/marine advice, and arrange a transmission-focused follow-up meeting.

These points condense the two source summaries and reflect the meeting record, including preliminary views and pending decisions.
